Fridgedoor

Privacy

Fridgedoor is software you run yourself. The people who make it never receive your household's data.

Where your data lives

Everything (plans, lists, email-derived suggestions, account details) is stored in one database on the server your household runs. Email content, private plans, passwords and keys are encrypted there. Whoever runs that server can access it, and usually that's you.

What leaves your server

  • Your AI provider, the one your household picked, receives what you send the planner and, for the morning brief, your upcoming plans and to-dos. It also receives email, one message at a time and trimmed, from members who turned on email suggestions. Each provider has its own data policy.
  • Your email provider, which Fridgedoor reads from. Fridgedoor never sends, deletes or marks mail as read.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it.
  • Push notification services (Apple, Google, Mozilla) carry short, generic notification text, if you turn notifications on.

The iPhone app

The app talks only to your household's server. It collects no analytics and contains no tracking or advertising code.

This website

fridgedoor.dev is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, with no cookies or analytics.

The full technical details are in docs/privacy.md.
