Fridgedoor

The family fridge door, as an app.

Fridgedoor reads the school newsletters, team emails, flyers and invitations in your inbox and turns them into a calm shared plan: what's on today, what needs a decision, and the lists you both use.

Set it up See the code
  • Reads email for youGmail, iCloud, Fastmail or any mailbox. Clear everyday logistics become plans; anything personal or uncertain stays private until you share it.
  • A morning briefToday's plans and anything waiting on a decision, in one place. Interruptions are rare and same-day only.
  • Ask the plannerType, speak, snap a flyer or share a PDF: "dinner with the Parkers Friday 7pm", "add oat milk".
  • Shared listsGroceries sorted by store aisle and to-dos with owners, for everyone in the household.
  • An iPhone appWidgets, Siri, the share sheet and reminders, plus a web app for everything else.
  • Any AI, your serverOpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter or a model on your own computer. Your data stays in one file on a server you control.

Set it up

Run it on a computer at home, a free cloud server or a small paid host. One command installs it and gives you a setup link; the rest happens in your browser.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ravimody/fridgedoor/main/deploy/install.sh | bash

Not sure where to run it? The hosting guide compares the options, and you can hand the AI setup guide to Claude Code or Codex and let it do the work.

Private by design

There's no Fridgedoor company in the middle and no tracking. Your household's data lives on your server and goes only to the AI provider and email account you choose. How privacy works.
