Fridgedoor

A household planner you run yourself.

Fridgedoor reads the school newsletters, team emails, flyers and invitations in your inbox and turns them into a shared plan for your household, next to your grocery and to-do lists.

Set it up See the code

  • Reads email for you

    Gmail, iCloud, Fastmail or any IMAP mailbox. Clear everyday logistics become suggestions for the household; anything personal or uncertain stays private until you share it.

  • A morning brief

    Today's plans and anything waiting on a decision, in one place. Interruptions are rare and only for things due the same day.

  • Ask the planner

    Type, speak, or attach a photo of a flyer or a PDF: "dinner with the Parkers Friday 7pm", "add oat milk".

  • Shared lists

    Grocery and to-do lists for everyone in the household.

  • On every phone

    A web app anyone can add to their Home Screen. There is also an iPhone app with widgets, Siri, the share sheet, reminders and an Apple Watch app, for now only for people who build it themselves with Xcode.

  • Any AI, your server

    OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter or a model on your own computer. Your data stays in one file on a server you control.

Set it up

Run it on a computer at home, a free cloud server or a small paid host. One command installs it and gives you a setup link, and you do the rest in your browser.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ravimody/fridgedoor/main/deploy/install.sh | bash

The hosting guide compares where to run it. You can also give the AI setup guide to Claude Code or Codex and let it do the setup.

Privacy

There's no Fridgedoor company in the middle and no tracking. Your household's data lives on your server. It goes only to services your household chooses: your AI provider and email account, and Google Calendar and phone notification services if you use them. How privacy works.
